# KGP Vibes Cafe: frequently asked questions

## When are you open?

Every day, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, at both outlets.

## Where are you?

- Koramangala: No. 27, 6th Cross, Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru 560095
- Indiranagar: 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 (opening early December 2026)

## Do you have vegetarian food?

Yes. Every item on the menu is vegetarian except the Startup Burger. The Talkie Tacos come with paneer or chicken;
ask for paneer.

## What is vegan?

Vibes Espresso Shot, KGP Cold Brew, Nitro Cold Brew, Cloud Fries, Algorithm Wrap, Server Side Salad, Backend Bowl,
Chill Zone Mojito and Bandwidth Lemonade. Coffee drinks with milk can be made with oat milk on request.

## Which items contain egg?

Byte Brownie, Python Pancakes, AI Waffle, Loop Croissant, Git Commit Cookies, Zero-Bug Brownie Sundae,
Compile Cheesecake and Runtime Tiramisu. Everything else is egg-free.

## What goes well together?

- Talkie Chai with the KGP Maggi Bowl or the Loop Croissant
- Tech Latte with the Byte Brownie
- KGP Cold Brew with the Binary Bagel
- Startup Burger with Cloud Fries and a Chill Zone Mojito
- API Affogato as the end of any meal

## Can I work on my laptop?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free (the password is on the table cards) and most seats have a power socket. At the busy hours
(8 to 10 AM and 1 to 2 PM) we ask laptop users to share tables.

## How can I pay?

UPI, card or cash. Prices include tax.

## Can I order ahead?

Soon. The new website lets you choose items and a pickup time. Until then, order at the counter.

## Do you take bookings?

Yes, for groups of 6 or more, birthdays and office catering. Email bookings@kgpvibescafe.example.com.

## Is there parking?

Two-wheeler parking in front of the cafe. Car parking is on the street.
