## The story in the numbers

- Sales are about 10,000 USD a month, from about 40 to 45 orders a day at about 8 USD each.
- The cafe keeps about 15 to 20% of sales as profit after ingredients, wages, rent and other costs.
- Talkie Chai is the best seller; coffee with a snack is the most common order.
- The busiest hours are 8 to 10 AM, which is where the long queue and most complaints come from.
