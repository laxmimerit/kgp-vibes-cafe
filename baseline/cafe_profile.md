## What works today

- Regulars love the Talkie Chai, the KGP Cold Brew and the calm, laptop-friendly space.
- Most reviews mention the vibe and the tech-themed menu names.

## What does not work today

- The 8 to 10 AM queue is long; some customers walk away.
- The printed menu is plain text, with no photos, and is often out of date.
- There is no website, so new customers cannot see the menu before they visit.
- The owner spends his evenings answering email, taking bookings and keeping the accounts.
- Staff answer the same questions all day: veg options, egg-free items, pairings, opening hours, Wi-Fi.
