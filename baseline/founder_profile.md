# Founder: Laxmi Kant

Laxmi Kant is the founder and owner of KGP Vibes Cafe.

He studied at IIT Kharagpur and spent more than ten years working in technology before he opened the cafe in 2025.
Over those years he noticed that the best ideas came out of long conversations over a cup of chai, not out of
meeting rooms. KGP Vibes Cafe is his answer to that: a calm, friendly place where students, developers and anyone
with an idea can sit, talk and work.

He still teaches online in his spare time, which is why the cafe has fast Wi-Fi, plenty of power sockets and a menu
full of tech jokes.

## What he cares about

- Good chai and good coffee at a fair price.
- A place where people feel welcome to stay a while.
- Treating his team well and knowing his regulars by name.

## What he wants next

The first outlet in Koramangala is busy and loved, but it runs on paper and memory. With the second outlet opening
in Indiranagar, he wants both cafes to look professional, be easy to find online, and take less of his evenings, so
he can spend more time with his customers and his team.
