# New outlet brief: KGP Vibes Cafe, Indiranagar

## Why a second outlet

Koramangala is full at the busy hours and many customers come from offices across town. A second outlet on
100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, brings the cafe closer to them. It opens in early December 2026 with the same menu.

## What is new

The owner wants the new outlet to launch with a professional look, and to bring the Koramangala outlet onto the same
look at the same time.

| Need | What we will make |
| --- | --- |
| People should find us online | A website: home, about, menu with photos, cart and a demo checkout with a pickup time |
| Shorter morning queue | Order ahead on the website: pick items, choose a pickup time, get an order number |
| A better printed menu | A new menu card that matches the website, with a QR code to the online menu |
| Something to post at launch | Food photos and a 15-second promo video |
| Fewer repeated questions | Barista Bot, an assistant that answers questions about the menu and the cafe |
| Less admin in the evenings | Email drafts, calendar bookings, a sales sheet and a monthly review deck |

## What is not part of this launch

- Real online payments. The checkout is a demo for now; a payment provider comes later.
- Staff logins, delivery apps or stock tracking.

## How we will know it worked

- Customers can see the full menu with photos before they visit.
- A customer can place a demo order on a phone in under a minute and sees a pickup time.
- One change to the menu updates both the website and the printed card.
- The owner's daily admin takes minutes, not an hour.
