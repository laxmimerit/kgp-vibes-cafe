# KGP Vibes Cafe: Indiranagar Launch Plan

## 1. The Goal
Open the second outlet on 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar to bring KGP Vibes closer to office workers and alleviate peak-hour crowding at Koramangala. Launch both locations under a unified, professional brand identity, introduce an order-ahead demo to cut morning queue times, and automate evening administrative overhead.

---

## 2. What We Build and In What Order

### Week -4: Brand Foundation & Creative Production
1. **Brand Brief & Style Guide**: Establish unified typography, colors, and visual tone across both outlets.
2. **Visual Content**: Produce professional photos for all 39 menu items and a 15-second launch promo video.
3. **Structured Menu Data**: Establish a single source of truth for menu items, pricing, and dietary tags.

### Week -3: Core Web Presence & Barista Bot
4. **Responsive Website**: Build Home, About, and Menu pages featuring food photography.
5. **Barista Bot**: Implement an AI assistant on the website to answer recurring customer questions about the cafe, location, and menu.

### Week -2: Order-Ahead Experience & Operational Setup
6. **Order-Ahead Flow (Demo)**: Build a lightweight cart and demo checkout allowing customers to select items, pick a pickup time, and receive an order number in under a minute.
7. **Admin Automation**: Configure email response templates, calendar booking workflows, a daily sales tracking sheet, and a monthly review deck.

### Week -1: Physical Collateral & Web Deployment
8. **Print Collateral**: Print new menu cards and table cards with QR codes linking directly to the live online menu.
9. **Website Go-Live**: Deploy the production website live and verify end-to-end user flows on mobile.
10. **Marketing Setup**: Configure targeted Instagram promotions ($300) and prepare the opening day "free chai with any order" campaign ($500).

### Opening Week: Launch
11. **Doors Open**: Open Indiranagar for service, activate the launch offers, and transition the Koramangala outlet to the new matching menu cards.

> **Out of Scope for Launch**: Real payment gateways (demo checkout only), staff login systems, third-party delivery integrations, and live inventory tracking.

---

## 3. How We Will Know It Worked
- **Menu Visibility**: Customers can browse the full 39-item menu with photos online before visiting.
- **Queue Reduction**: A customer can complete a mobile order-ahead demo with a designated pickup time in under 60 seconds.
- **Unified Updates**: A single update to the menu data updates both the website and the printed menu card.
- **Admin Relief**: Daily administrative overhead (inbox, bookings, sales logs) takes minutes rather than an hour.
- **Budget Discipline**: The entire launch is delivered within the $1,500 total budget.
